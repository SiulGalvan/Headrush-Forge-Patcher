# Your rack-module art (optional)

The 11 rack-style modules (AIR Delay, Detune, Dyn Delay, DynIII Comp, Eleven Reverb, Multi Chorus,
Noise Filter, Para EQ, Side Comp, Smart Harm, Tape Echo) ship as thin strips in the firmware.
With **Pedal design** enabled you can give them a pedal-shaped picture: put PNGs with transparency here
(or pick any folder in the GUI / `--rack-art DIR`), named after the module, e.g. `Eleven Reverb.png`,
`SIDE COMP.png`, `tapeecho.png` (case, spaces and punctuation do not matter; `Tape Delay` also works for Tape Echo).

Each image is scaled so the pedal body is 396 px tall on a 286x442 canvas, centred, then pre-scaled and
stored as a palette PNG (it must fit the slot of the original image). Modules without a picture keep the
stock look. No pictures are shipped with this project: the artwork belongs to its owners.
